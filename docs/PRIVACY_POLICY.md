# Privacy Policy

**BeatFlare** — Last updated: May 6, 2026

## What the app does

BeatFlare captures audio from your device's microphone to visualize music on the Nothing Phone glyph LEDs and/or the front screen in real time.

## Data collection

BeatFlare does **not** collect, store, record, or transmit any data. Specifically:

- **Microphone audio** is processed entirely on-device in real time and immediately discarded. No audio is ever recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.
- **No personal data** is collected, stored, or shared.
- **No analytics, tracking, or advertising** SDKs are included.
- **No network access** is used. The app works fully offline.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it's needed |
|---|---|
| `RECORD_AUDIO` | Captures live microphone input for real-time music visualization. Audio is processed in memory and never stored. |
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` | Keeps the visualizer running when the screen is off. |
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Shows a persistent notification with playback controls while the service is running. |

## Third-party services

None. BeatFlare has no third-party dependencies that process user data.

## Children's privacy

BeatFlare does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in this repository.
